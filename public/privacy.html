Privacy

Zombie Ants · last updated 4 September 2026

Zombie Ants does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up and no server holding anything about you. Everything the game remembers is stored on your own device.

What the game stores, on your device

Your save is written to your device's own app storage — browser storage on the web, Android application storage in the installed app. It contains:

  • the colony name you chose, and the size of your colony;
  • your mycelium, pheromone and larva, and what you have bought with them;
  • your settings, your progress, and the matches you have played;
  • a player code, generated on your device so a support message can be matched to a save.

None of it is sent anywhere. It is readable only by the game on that device, and it is deleted when you reset your colony in Settings or uninstall the app.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. Nothing is measured or sent.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No trackers, no third-party cookies and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no files.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

When the device does talk to the internet

There are three, and only three:

  • Loading the game. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which — like any web server — records the request, including your IP address, in its own logs. See GitHub's privacy statement. The installed Android app is served from inside the app and does not do this.
  • Fonts. The page loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so Google receives the request. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Checking for a newer version. The web version fetches a small file to see whether a newer build has been published, so it can reload onto it rather than leaving you on a stale page. It sends nothing about you.

The game itself is played entirely on your device. Matches are against the computer; nothing about them leaves the device.

If you contact support

The Support screen writes your message into your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press send there, and you can see exactly what it contains: your message, the build number, and your player code. If you email us, we have your message and your email address — which we use only to answer you.

Purchases

Where the game sells anything, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details, and no payment information is kept on your device beyond what you bought.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anybody, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
