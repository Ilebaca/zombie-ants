How to play
Goal: capture the enemy nest (♛). One action per turn, 15s per move.
Move / Attack a neighbour — the tile you take becomes a producing stable. Wild grey colonies must be beaten to pass.
Travel sends troops across a path; the trail it lays stays vein until you reinforce it. You can also travel onto your own vein to promote it.
Rally gathers all your troops onto one tile; Advance rallies them to your front line.
Tiles cut off from your nest are frozen — no troops, no growth — until you reconnect them.
The Hive wakes mid‑game and hardens over time. Take the Queen for a surge that grows stronger every capture.
The match runs until one colony's nest falls.
